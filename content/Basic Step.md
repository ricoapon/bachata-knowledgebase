---
title: Basic Step
---

The leader starts with the left foot: step, close, step, tap. Then you do this again the other direction. So in total it takes 8 counts.

![YouTube Video](https://youtu.be/FTxXiQfUHUg)

Another basic is to move forwards instead of sidewards. This is walking forwards with 3 steps, tap, walk backwards with 3 steps, tap. Again, it takes 8 counts.

![YouTube Video](https://youtu.be/mGoI0_5Xe8U)
