tags:
  - learned
---

The leader makes a step forward while the follower makes it backwards. The crucial part is that the torso is completely tilted. Easier to do from [[Closed Position]].

The Forward Step is done in 4 counts and as a preparation for other moves. The video shows it being done twice.
