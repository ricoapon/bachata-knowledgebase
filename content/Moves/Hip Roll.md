The leader indicates a movement with the hips by pulling on follower to make the hips move forwards. Then the follower can make a round motion back.

![Beginners 2 Lesson 2](https://youtu.be/vCkGPeJ5U14)

Note that this is logical to do from a [[Hammerlock]], which gives a good hold for moving the hips. There are other positions where you can use your hands directly on the followers hips for this move. See the move below.

![Beginners 2 Lesson 3](https://youtu.be/4IkzSoFBQ3I)
