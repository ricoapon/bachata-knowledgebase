Grab the wrists of the follower and move the arms down. Let go, so the follower moves the arm up.

![Beginners 3 Lesson 3](https://youtu.be/nuUA7lD2LZA)

You can also do this from [[Shadow Position]], as long as the arms are wrapped.

![Nova Dance 2016-10-14](https://youtu.be/IK3Jl8GfeFs)
