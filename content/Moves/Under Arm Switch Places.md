title: Under Arm Switch Places
tags:
  - learned
---

- [[Turn]] for the leader into [[Reverse Crosshold]]
- [[Turn]] for the follower, holding both hands, into a [[Hammerlock]]. Do a [[Hair Comb]], putting your hand on the back of the follower under the neck.
- Step side/backwards and push the follower forward. Raise your elbow, so the follower can duck under. Step forwards yourself and you will both turn to each other.

You can do this both on count 1 and 5. Doing it from count 5 feels a bit more natural to me.
