This refers to the 4 first counts to leave the [[Shadow Position]] with some kind of turn. You prepare the follower.

The move: step forwards with left, back with right and then side/backwards with left. Tap. Make sure to lead the last step with your left arm, so the follower is really prepared for a turn.
