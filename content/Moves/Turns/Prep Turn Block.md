---
title: Prep Turn Block
tags:
  - learned
---

Do a [[Prep Turn]], but when the follower is half-way her turn, block her with your hands on her hip.

The video shows standing still after the block, but it is very common to push the follower to the other side to make a step on 7. Then you end up in a [[Reverse Shadow Position]].
